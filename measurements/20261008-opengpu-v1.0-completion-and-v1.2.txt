What stays on the CPU, and why:
  pixels (ORTG_WritePixels) small pieces: 3-byte RGB and the like, which no
      v1.0 format describes, and ARGB into 32-bit bitmaps (OpenRTG keeps
      alpha 0 there; PIXELS would keep the source's)
  blit planar: icons and images in planar bitmaps (v1.0 has no planar)
  blit masked: BltMaskBitMapRastPort, through a one-bit mask
  lines that are not across, down or at 45 degrees: OpenGPU's LINE and
      OpenRTG's line() break ties differently (4960 of 14641 short lines
      differ), so only lines without ties go there
  patterned lines (LinePtrn), WritePixel and ReadPixel, ReadPixelArray8

The 68k's JIT
-------------
Register shifts cost about 70 ns a bit of count on the AC090 (a shift by 24
nearly 2 us), and mulu.l, divu.l, bfextu and bfins 70-90 ns: noted for the
JIT's owner. opengpu.library's core avoids them in its pixel loops (see
ogpu_core.c's header); OpenGPUBench before and after is in #27.
