SDL 2 on AmigaOS 3.2: the CPU against OpenGPU -- 8 October 2026

Machine
-------
A scratch copy of the AmigaChrome Showcase instance (OS 3.2.3, AC090 68040
JIT, ACRTG), Workbench on OpenRTG at 800x600x32, OpenUp 0.6.16, AHI 6 with
acaudio.audio. Runtime and ACRTG boot ROM (acrtg.card 2.3) as in the Mesa
lab. opengpu.library 0.4 (stream v1.2, branch opengpu/stream-v12-20261008)
with LIBS:OpenGPU/ACRTG.gpu 1.0.

Under OpenRTG nothing yet places the ACRTG board's command ring (acrtg.card
2.3 does it under Picasso96), so a lab-only tool wrote RING_BASE and
RING_SIZE as the card would (64 KiB at the top 256 KiB of video RAM) before
anything opened opengpu.library. Without it ACRTG.gpu steps aside and
OpenGPU draws on the 68k.

Programs: SDL 2.32.10's testsprite2 and testrendercopyex, unchanged, on
SDL2.module (the .m programs), 640x480 windows on the Workbench, 20 s each
(tests/sdl2/bench). Frames per second as the tests print them, every 5 s.

  CPU      SDL_OPENGPU=0: SDL's software renderer; the window shown with
           WritePixelArray. No OpenGPU anywhere.
  OpenGPU  the opengpu renderer; the window shown by OpenGPU into video RAM.

The grey JPEG was GCC 16, not the AC090
---------------------------------------
tiny_jpeg built at -O2 wrote the same flat grey picture on the current
runtime, the new JIT (amigachrome #293), the new JIT with JIT_NOOPS020=1 and
the interpreter alone (JIT_INTERP=1). In tjei_encode_and_write_MCU,
GCC 16's -m68040 float-to-int saves FPCR in d0, the register that already
held the zig-zag index, then stores through it:
    move.b (a3)+,d0            the index
    fmovem.l fpcr,d0           FPCR saved over it
    ...
    fmove.l fp0,(a5,d0.l*4)    every coefficient to du[0]
-m68020 -m68881 and -m68060 use fintrz and don't clash. fpcr_check.py finds
it in a built file; SDL2.module has none, the satellites had five (Timidity),
now built at -O0 with IMG_jpg.c.
