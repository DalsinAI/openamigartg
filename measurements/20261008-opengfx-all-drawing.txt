OpenGfx owns every drawing call: opengpu.library 0.8, openrtg.library 0.13
8 October 2026, a scratch copy of the OpenUp 0.6.17 lab instance (OS 3.2.3,
AC090 68040, ACRTG), never a live instance. OpenLook 0.6 (opengadtools main,
with the depth fix), Open theme, light. Workbench at 800 x 600 on:

  Picasso96 32-bit   mode $510A1300 (no OpenRTG; OpenLook puts OpenGfx's patches in)
  Picasso96 16-bit   mode $510A1102
  the OpenRTG part   mode $61011100, 16-bit (openrtg.library registers as OpenGfx's provider)

Before: opengpu.library 0.7 + openrtg.library 0.12 (main). After: opengpu.library
0.8 + openrtg.library 0.13 (this branch). Picasso96 also against OpenUp 0.6.17 as
shipped (OpenLook 0.5, opengpu.library 0.6: no OpenGfx patches at all).

Each boot: OpenPrefs Look, SYS: and MultiView (a PNG) opened, a screenshot;
the pointer, driven from outside the Amiga, opens MultiView's menu, a screenshot; GfxPatches ALL;
GfxBench N=60 three times; OpenGfxCheck QUICK; on OpenRTG, OpenRTGExact.
Three boots of each configuration, interleaved.

Patches (opengfx-all-20261008/gfxpatches-*.txt, GfxPatches ALL from opengadtools):
  after, all three:       all 22 calls, exactly one Open patch each: OpenGfx's.
                          On Picasso96 its own patches sit behind OpenGfx's on
                          some calls (BltPattern, the pixel calls, the chunky

Timings (opengfx-all-20261008/timings-*.txt): GfxBench, microseconds a call,
the best of nine runs (three boots, three runs each). The machine was busy
(load 17 to 33 on 12 cores, other builds running), so differences under about
15 per cent are noise. No call is slower beyond that: on Picasso96 32-bit and
on the OpenRTG part every line is within the noise or faster; on Picasso96
16-bit the largest after/before ratios are Text 1 character x100 (223 / 194),
20 slanted lines (83 / 72) and BltTemplate (75 / 62), within the noise of the
same lines at 32 bits, where after is the faster. A pass-through costs
OpenGfx one entry and one C call on top of the patch below it.
